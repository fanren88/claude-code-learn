**Claude Code** is Anthropic's AI coding assistant. It reads your repo, proposes edits, runs commands, and helps with Git—from the terminal, desktop app, IDE extensions, and more.

## Who is it for?

Developers who want an agentic coding assistant on **their own machine**, with control over data and workflow.
